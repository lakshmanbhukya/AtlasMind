\section{Deterministic Ambiguity Gating}
\label{sec:gate}

To eliminate silent assumptions without adding multiple seconds of auxiliary language-model pre-filtering latency, AMB-MQL employs an in-process, deterministic ambiguity gate prior to generation.

\subsection{Trigger Terms and Metric Extraction}
The gate evaluates natural language requests against a fixed set of 15 trigger terms:
\begin{align*}
\mathcal{T}_{\text{vague}} = \{ & \text{top}, \text{best}, \text{highest}, \text{lowest}, \text{most}, \text{worst}, \text{greatest}, \\
& \text{least}, \text{cheapest}, \text{priciest}, \text{maximum}, \text{minimum}, \\
& \text{max}, \text{min}, \text{largest} \}.
\end{align*}
When a word boundary match occurs, the gate introspects the profiled schemas of collections in scope to extract numeric metric candidates ($\mathcal{F}_{\text{num}}$). Candidate qualification inspects the trailing segment of dot-paths, accepting numeric BSON types (\texttt{number}, \texttt{Double}, \texttt{Int32}, \texttt{Long}, \texttt{Decimal128}) while filtering out identifier-like (\texttt{\_id}, \texttt{customerId}) and year-like (\texttt{year}, \texttt{fiscalYear}) fields.

\subsection{Heuristic Field Binding and Clarification Cards}
If $|\mathcal{F}_{\text{num}}| \ge 2$, the query presents multiple plausible quantitative metrics. The gate inspects the prompt for explicit binding phrases (e.g., ``by price'', ``based on rating''). When no explicit binding is detected, the gate emits an interactive clarification card with up to four candidate fields truncated alphabetically or by sample order, plus an explicit ``Other'' option.

\subsection{Execution Latency}
Because the gate executes entirely in-process using compiled regular expressions and cached schema dictionaries, its runtime overhead is negligible. In microbenchmarking across 10,000 iterations on host hardware (AMD Ryzen 5 5600H), the gate achieved a mean execution latency of \gateLatencyMean{} (median: \gateLatencyPFifty{}, 99th percentile: \gateLatencyPNineNine{}). Compared to model generation latencies ($\sim$18--19\,s), the gate introduces virtually zero latency overhead (0.00018\%).
