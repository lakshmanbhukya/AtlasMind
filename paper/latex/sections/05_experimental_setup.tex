\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\subsection{Benchmark Construction and Datasets}
We evaluate AMB-MQL on a benchmark of \numTotalQueries{} execution-verified queries spanning three MongoDB Atlas sample datasets:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \texttt{sample\_supplies} (sales records with nested line-item arrays, pricing, and customer demographics).
    \item \texttt{sample\_analytics} (financial trading accounts, customer transactions, and portfolio limits).
    \item \texttt{sample\_mflix} (movie catalog, critic reviews, awards, and user commentary across multiple collections).
\end{itemize}
Queries are stratified across five distinct semantic categories:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \texttt{AMB\_LEX}: Queries containing lexical vagueness terms where the underlying collection schema offers $\ge 2$ plausible numeric candidates.
    \item \texttt{AMB\_PARA}: Queries expressing ambiguity through natural language paraphrase without trigger vocabulary.
    \item \texttt{CLR\_FIELD}: Unambiguous queries that explicitly identify the target metric despite dense candidate schemas.
    \item \texttt{CLR\_BOUND}: Unambiguous queries specifying explicit bounds, limits, or ordinal constraints.
    \item \texttt{CLR\_PLAIN}: Standard unambiguous queries (e.g., projection, counting, exact matching).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Human Annotation and Inter-Annotator Agreement}
Ground truth ambiguity was established through independent human annotation. Two annotators (Student 1 with intermediate MongoDB experience and Student 2 with beginner knowledge) independently labeled queries under a shared annotation guide without code inspection. Double-labeling was conducted on a designated stratified sample of \numDoubleLabelStratified{} queries. Disagreements and edge cases were adjudicated through consensus.

On the \numDoubleLabelStratified{} designated stratified rows, the annotators achieved \observedAgreementStratified{} observed agreement, yielding a Cohen's kappa of $\kappa = \cohenKappaStratified{}$ and a Prevalence-Adjusted Bias-Adjusted Kappa (PABAK) of \pabakStratified{}. Across all \numDoubleLabelAll{} double-labeled rows in the corpus, observed agreement was \observedAgreementAll{} ($\kappa = \cohenKappaAll{}$, $\text{PABAK} = \pabakAll{}$), confirming high inter-annotator reliability. The finalized benchmark contains \numAmbiguousQueries{} ambiguous queries and \numClearQueries{} clear queries.

\subsection{Evaluation Configurations and LLM Infrastructure}
For end-to-end evaluation, we select a representative stratified subset of \e2eTotalQueries{} queries (\e2eAmbiguousQueries{} ambiguous, \e2eClearQueries{} clear) and evaluate three generation configurations:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{C0a (Zero-ARL Baseline):} Standard single-pass generation with clarification disabled (\texttt{disableClarificationMode: true}), measuring raw baseline silent assumption susceptibility.
    \item \textbf{C0b (LLM-Only Baseline):} Conversational generation where the model autonomously chooses between synthesizing a query or requesting clarification (\texttt{needsClarification: true}).
    \item \textbf{C2 (AtlasMind Dual-Tier):} In-process deterministic gate pre-filter $\to$ allowlist-validated clarification card $\to$ single-call generation fallback.
\end{itemize}
Evaluations are conducted on the open-weights frontier model \texttt{openai/gpt-oss-120b} hosted on Groq LPU infrastructure under a single API key budget discipline.
