\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Natural Language Interfaces to Databases}
Natural Language Interfaces to Databases (NLIDBs) have been extensively studied within declarative relational paradigms~\cite{quamar2022nli,ozcan2020nli}. Standardized benchmarks such as Spider~\cite{spider2018} and BIRD~\cite{bird2023} catalyzed the development of decomposed prompting strategies, in-context self-correction, and modular generation architectures such as DIN-SQL~\cite{dinsql2023} and DAIL-SQL~\cite{dailsql2023}. These relational systems benefit from strict Data Definition Language (DDL) schemas and declarative relational optimizers that decouple semantic intent from physical query execution paths.

\subsection{Text-to-NoSQL and MongoDB Translation}
In contrast to relational Text-to-SQL, document-oriented translation must synthesize procedural aggregation pipelines composed of sequence-dependent transformation stages~\cite{mongodb2024manual}. Early supervised approaches by Hossen et al.~\cite{hossen2023bert} and Blank et al.~\cite{blank2019nosql} employed fine-tuned BERT representations to predict elementary CRUD operations. Recent benchmarks have expanded evaluation to cross-domain MongoDB query translation: DocSpider~\cite{docspider2025} established cross-domain natural language querying datasets for document collections, SM3-Text~\cite{sm3text2024} explored multi-model medical query synthesis, and Lu et al. introduced TEND~\cite{tend2025benchmark} and MultiTEND~\cite{multitend2025} to measure execution accuracy on complex aggregation pipelines. TEND established that frontier LLMs achieve less than 36\% mean execution accuracy on unconstrained MongoDB aggregation, emphasizing the unique challenges posed by document polymorphism, array unwinding, and procedural pipeline composition.

\subsection{Ambiguity Resolution in Conversational Systems}
Detecting and clarifying user ambiguity has a rich foundation in conversational information retrieval and question answering. Aliannejadi et al.~\cite{aliannejadi2019asking} and Zamani et al.~\cite{zamani2020generating} investigated clarification questions for open-domain web search, while Min et al. developed AmbigQA~\cite{min2020ambigqa} to study multi-answer distributions under polysemous questions. In interactive visual analytics, Setlur et al. introduced Eviza~\cite{setlur2016eviza} to support conversational visual querying, and Fast et al. designed Iris~\cite{fast2018iris} to execute complex conversational data-science workflows. AMB-MQL bridges conversational ambiguity resolution and database-systems architecture, demonstrating that in-process, deterministic schema gating provides a sub-millisecond, grounded alternative to multi-pass language model chains.
